# Quiet Frame

A design movement for marks that must survive at eighty pixels on a television and still feel composed
at a metre wide. The frame is the subject: a rounded rectangle that holds light. Everything else exists to
give that frame weight, warmth and a horizon. The work must read as meticulously crafted, the product of
deep expertise and countless small refinements, never as a glyph picked from a sheet.

**Space and form.** One silhouette, closed and calm. Shapes are built from a single family of radii so that
every corner belongs to the same hand. Strokes are heavy enough to hold at icon size, and the negative
space inside them is treated as a drawn element, not leftover. The horizon is the only line that curves;
all other geometry is orthogonal or circular. Nothing touches the edge of its tile; the mark floats inside
a margin that is itself a measured decision.

**Colour and material.** A deep, almost black blue is the ground. On it, a paper-white frame, a sky in one
calm blue, and a single disc of warm amber: the sun, the one point of heat. Green appears only as the low
mass of a hill, darker than the sky, so the image reads as a landscape even when it is nine pixels tall.
No gradients that cannot be printed in two inks; no shadows that pretend to be lighting. Material is flat,
exact, painstakingly aligned.

**Scale and rhythm.** The mark is designed small first. Every proportion is checked at 80 and 130 pixels
before it is admired at 1024. The wordmark sits beside or below the mark in a geometric sans with even
rhythm, letter-spacing opened a fraction so that it breathes at distance; the word is one calm gesture, not
two words fighting. Text is a whisper of identity, never explanation.

**Composition and hierarchy.** The frame leads, the sun anchors, the hill grounds, the word confirms.
Weight sits slightly low, like a photograph standing on a shelf. Symmetry is avoided by a hair so that the
composition feels placed by a person. The whole must look as though a master of the craft spent hours
moving things by single pixels until the stillness was right.
