# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH. Every release is a git tag `vX.Y.Z`. The Docker image carries the same version.

## Unreleased

- Android 0.2.11: on a TV, holding OK in the viewer is the Best Foto heart (a remote without colour buttons, such as the Chromecast's, has no yellow button); a short OK still starts and pauses the slideshow, now at release. The caption shows the hint.

## 0.5.0 – 2026-11-15

- The Best Foto heart (web, Android 0.2.10, LG TV 0.1.8). Every photo and video in the viewer has a heart: an outline when it does not have the tag "Best Foto", filled when it has. An administrator's heart adds or removes the tag at once (and writes it to Synology Photos when `SYNO_WRITE_TAGS=1`). Any other signed-in user files a suggestion: their heart fills at once, drawn dashed while it waits, and a second press takes the suggestion back; other people see nothing until it is approved. **Settings, Best Foto** lists the suggested photos with who suggested them, and the administrator approves or rejects them, one by one or all at once. Only the administrator takes a photo out of Best Foto. Guests and share links have no heart. Marked photos carry a small heart in the grid. In the web the key is `b`, on a TV remote the yellow button. Suggestions follow moved and renamed files like tags do. The tag's name can be changed with the setting `best_tag`.
- API: every item carries `info.best` (0 none, 1 tagged, 2 my suggestion waits); `SYNO.PhotoStation.Best` `set` (`id`, `value`) on `best.php`; admin operations `best_list`, `best_approve`, `best_reject`.

## 0.4.0 – 2026-11-15

- Tags can be written back to Synology Photos. With `SYNO_WRITE_TAGS=1` (and the people sync set up), a tag added or removed on a photo in the main gallery is added or removed in the shared space of Synology Photos as well, through its API; nothing is written into the files. The tag in the gallery is saved in any case, and the web says so when Synology Photos could not be written or does not have the file yet. Private galleries are not written: the service account does not see their personal space. Off by default.
- Collections in the apps (Android 0.2.8, LG TV 0.1.7), laid out like the web's Collections tab: a Collections tile on the start screen opens the smart albums followed by People, Tags and Places; tags are listed by name, places by country and then by the number of photos, and a tag, person, place or smart album opens its photos newest first, with the viewer and the slideshow as in an album. The People, Tags and Places tiles and the countries show the cover of their biggest entry (the web shows a collage of four). Creating and editing smart albums stays in the web.
- Live Photos in the TV apps (Android 0.2.5, LG TV 0.1.5): a tile shows LIVE, and when the focus rests on it the motion plays once, muted, in the tile; OK opens the photo full screen without playing it again; moving to a Live Photo in the viewer (arrows, swipe, slideshow) plays its motion once over the photo. On a phone the viewer has a LIVE button that plays the motion with sound. Android 0.2.6 draws the motion in a TextureView, so it stays inside its tile. Android 0.2.7 downloads the motions around the focus and the photo in view ahead into a 100 MB clip cache, so they start at once (ordinary videos are still not prefetched). In a slideshow (Android 0.2.7, LG TV 0.1.6) the zoom and the interval of a Live Photo start when its motion has played, so the zoomed photo does not show beside the clip.

## 0.3.0 – 2026-11-01

- Tags from Synology Photos: the run that takes people from the shared space also takes its general tags (source `synology` in `item_tags`). A tag added in Synology Photos appears on the photo, a tag removed there disappears; a Synology tag removed in the gallery is not added again. Keywords from the files and tags added in the gallery are not touched.
- LG TV app 0.1.4: renews LG's Developer Mode session (1000 hours) at its start, at most once a day, in the background, so the TV does not remove the app.
- Android app 0.2.4: only the video on screen has a player; the neighbouring pages of a video show its thumbnail and download nothing, and leaving a video releases its player at once (several 1080p HEVC videos at the same time made playback stutter on a Chromecast HD).

## 0.2.0 – 2026-11-01

- Android app 0.1.4: one app holds several paired galleries (for example the main gallery and a private `/~name` gallery of the same server), each paired with its own code; the other galleries appear as tiles on the start screen, Settings lists them with Add gallery and Remove. A slideshow on a TV slowly zooms every photo from fitting the screen to filling it (a portrait photo ends edge to edge); on a TV remote OK shows the caption and starts the slideshow, OK again pauses it. Earlier installs are converted without a new code.
- Android app 0.2.0: administration on phones and tablets for a user who is an administrator of the gallery (Settings, Administration), over the same `/api/admin` the web uses: TV (allow a code shown on a TV or phone, remove a device), Users (create, note, password, administrator, disabled, groups, delete), Groups, Without signing in (public root files, the guest's albums), the access editor per user, group or guest (allow, exclude, remove a rule, allow all inside) and, in an album, Access (who sees it and why) and Edit album (title, description, order, private / public / with password, cover reset). 0.2.1 adds Shares (links from the menu of an album: validity, downloading, note; copy or send; cancel), General (name, order, language, the twelve restrictions), Galleries (main gallery: create, administrators, on/off, delete) and Moves (ambiguous moves, settings of albums that are gone, history). 0.2.2 adds the viewer's menu: a share link for the photo or video, Set as album cover (any album above the photo) and Tags (add with the gallery's names offered, remove). Comments and smart albums stay in the web for now: the app has no views for them yet. On a TV the administration is not shown. 0.2.3 adds Rename for a paired device.
- Settings, TV: a paired device can be renamed (web and the Android app 0.2.3; `device_rename` in the admin API), so a TV is listed as "Living room" instead of the model it sent.
- Android app 0.1.5: updates install themselves. At the start the app looks at `/app/version.json`; on Android 12 and later, once installs from the app are allowed, a newer version is downloaded and installed without a dialog (an app may update itself: `UPDATE_PACKAGES_WITHOUT_USER_ACTION`, PackageInstaller session). Android then stops the app and does not let it start itself again, so the user opens the new version. Elsewhere the banner with Update stays, and the system's confirmation appears. (0.1.6: the silent path is used only over https and only after Android 12's own check; a redirect away from the server is refused, a download is capped at 100 MB and checked against the `sha256` that `publish.py` now writes into version.json; Android accepts only an APK signed with the app's own key in any case.)
- LG TV app 0.1.3: the same several galleries (tiles on the start screen, Settings with Add gallery and Remove, OK twice to remove) and the slideshow zoom; the TV's stored server is converted without a new code.
- Sideways videos on the TV: phones record portrait videos lying on their side and only set a rotation flag, which the TV's player ignores. The server now records the flag in the video's metadata (`video_codec.rotation`) and keeps an upright copy of such videos (H.264/AAC, at most 1080p) in `DATA/video_tv/`, made by ffmpeg in the background at the lowest priority, one at a time; after a start it goes through the library once, newest folders first. The TV app asks for `variant=tv` and gets the copy, or the original with the header `X-Picturestation-Video: preparing` until it exists (`video_codec.tv_version`). Needs the ffmpeg package, which the installers bring.
- `/app/` serves the files of `DATA/app` (for example the Android APK, so that a phone downloads it from the gallery's own address).
- Android app (`Android/`): one APK for phones, tablets and Android TV (Android 8.0 and later). It pairs with a code like the LG TV app, browses albums and the newest photos, shows photos full screen with zoom, people and place, plays videos (the player honours the rotation flag), runs slideshows and shares a photo; on a TV it has a launcher banner and works with the remote. The session travels in a header; plain http is used only when the user asks for it.
- LG TV app (`LGTV/`): a webOS app for LG TVs from webOS 3.0 on, installed through LG's Developer Mode; it browses albums and plays photos, slideshows and videos from the server over the DS photo API. The TV is paired like a streaming app: it shows a code, the administrator enters it under Settings, TV, and chooses whose photos the TV sees; the TV stays signed in until it is removed there. For it the API answers CORS requests from any origin, without credentials (a browser session stays in its cookie and is never sent cross-site), and a packaged webOS app (User-Agent `WebAppManager`) carries its session like the DS photo app: `X-SYNO-TOKEN` header, `PHPSESSID` in image URLs.
- Repository layout: the web gallery moved from `server/picturestation/web` to `web/` at the top of the repository. The installers and the Docker image put it in `/opt/picturestation/web`; `PICTURESTATION_WEB_DIR` points the server elsewhere. An update with `install.sh --update` handles it; a hand-made copy of the code must now include `web/` too.
- Viewer: typing a comment no longer triggers the viewer keys ("i" hid the details panel, arrows switched the photo).
- Access editor: "Allow all inside" allows an album and removes the principal's own rules in its sub-albums in one step (a confirmation lists them); group rules stay. After Allow/Exclude the album list and the Settings page stay where they were scrolled.
- Live Photos: an iPhone photo (HEIC/JPG) and its short .MOV with the same name in the same folder are one item. The video is left out of albums, the timeline, search and collections; the photo has a LIVE badge, its motion plays in the grid when the mouse rests on it, and over the photo in the viewer (mouse over LIVE, or a tap). The video can be downloaded from the photo details and goes with the photo in a share link. Videos longer than 5 seconds stay separate. Settings, General: on by default, can be turned off per gallery.
- Search for names with an underscore (for example IMG_1234) found nothing.
- EXIF from HEIC/HEIF and RAW photos (iPhone and camera raw) via exiftool, when it is installed: date, GPS/place, dimensions, camera and keywords. Thumbnails for these still come from the NAS (@eaDir). Needs the libimage-exiftool-perl package, now in the installers and the Docker image.
- Security hardening (pre-release audit): a share link or an unlocked album no longer bypasses a password-protected sub-album inside it; ready-made @eaDir thumbnails/videos are served only when they really resolve inside the gallery's photo root (a symlink in a user-writable archive cannot read outside it); the session cookie gets the Secure flag over HTTPS; the session id is accepted from the URL only for the DS photo app, never a browser; webapi responses send X-Content-Type-Options, pages send X-Frame-Options; paths with NUL/control bytes are rejected cleanly; a dead NFS gallery folder no longer exhausts the folder-check pool; per-gallery cover caches are no longer flushed across tenants.
- Private galleries: any number of separate photo archives in one server, managed by the main gallery's administrator under Settings, Galleries. Each has its own users, rights, thumbnails and database, and is reached at /~name/ or at its own domain. They replace the per-gallery services (`picturestation-instance`) of 0.1.0.
- Faster Collections page. Covers of tags, places, people and smart albums are remembered from the lists, the library list is rebuilt in the background and warmed up at start, and permissions are evaluated once per folder.
- Places also for villages below 500 inhabitants. `PICTURESTATION_GEONAMES_COUNTRIES=SK,CZ` downloads the full GeoNames lists of those countries, with populated places only and without city sections. Settlements are named after their municipality, so a part of a village shows the village name. After the place data changes, the gallery looks up the places of all photos again, once.
- The installers wait up to 10 minutes for the package manager lock, for example while unattended upgrades run, instead of stopping.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-10-21

First version.

- **DS photo app.** The Photo Station 6 API is supported: albums, timeline, tags, places, smart albums, comments, covers, thumbnails, downloads and video.
- **Web gallery.** It has albums, a timeline, search, a viewer with details, and collections of smart albums, tags, places and people. The interface is in English and Slovak.
- **Rights.** Users, groups and guests are supported. Album access is shown as a tree with allowed and excluded albums, and it can also be set per album. Albums can have passwords, and share links can expire.
- **Restrictions.** Downloads, GPS, camera details, comments, timeline and search can be limited, and photos in the root can be made public.
- **Tags and places.** Tags come from file keywords (IPTC/XMP). Places are looked up offline from GPS with GeoNames.
- **People from Synology Photos.** They are synchronised through a read-only service account.
- **Moved folders.** A move is recognised by the files, and rights, titles, covers, tags, comments, links and smart albums are carried over.
- **Importers.** Rights can be imported from Photo Station 6, and tags and albums from Synology Photos.
- **Personal galleries.** A separate instance can run for one person's folder.
- **Installation.** There are installers for Debian 13 and for Proxmox VE (LXC or VM), a Docker image and a compose example.
